# The Five Bacha Rouge Experiences

## 01 — The Moroccan Table

### Flavours rooted in tradition

Discover the authentic soul of Moroccan cuisine through fragrant tagines, carefully prepared couscous and traditional specialties. From chicken with preserved lemon to slow-cooked lamb shank and Marrakech Tanjia, every dish brings the richness of Moroccan culinary heritage to your table.

## 02 — Beldi, Reimagined

### Tradition meets creativity

A contemporary expression of Moroccan inspiration. Explore a selection of refined creations, from filet mignon with carob sauce and sea bream with regional herbs to our signature Bacha Mrouzia Burger. Familiar flavours take on unexpected forms.

## 03 — Sweet Moments

### An indulgence worth remembering

Let the evening unfold with delicate desserts and signature refreshments. Discover our San Sebastián cheesecake, amlou and almond fondant, saffron crème brûlée and Jawhara, accompanied by colourful mocktails and freshly prepared juices.

## 04 — The Red Room

### Where atmosphere becomes an experience

Step into a world of deep red velvet, golden lighting and distinctive details. Surrounded by elegant décor and an intimate atmosphere, the Red Room is a place for meaningful conversations, shared moments and evenings worth remembering.

## 05 — Above the Blue

### A different perspective of Chefchaouen

Rise above the everyday and discover our blue rooftop, where the surrounding mountains create a breathtaking backdrop. With open skies, beautiful views and a more relaxed atmosphere, it is the perfect setting to pause, unwind and enjoy the beauty of the Blue Pearl.
